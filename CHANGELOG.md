# Peer Review

## 0.9.2

- The popup only asks about players something actually happened with: you fought together, were in a dungeon or raid together, killed a boss, or completed a trade. Someone who just sat in your party is ignored, however long they stayed.

## 0.9.1

- Players you were grouped with for less than 5 minutes never show up, in the popup or anywhere else. No more popups after a server hop, a trade or a quick craft.

## 0.9.0

Everything from the 0.8.2 to 0.8.6 betas, now as a full release:

- When someone leaves your group while you stay, the popup asks about them right away.
- Skip button on every player, and a no-entry icon for "never ask me about this player".
- New thumb icons and the Peer Review logo on the minimap button.
- See your own rating under My ratings.
- Preview the popup from the settings, and drag any window where you want it. Positions are remembered.
- Feedback and bug report button in the settings.
- Sharing uses a hidden realm-wide channel automatically, so votes reach players outside your guild and group.

## 0.8.6

- When someone leaves your group while you stay, the popup asks about them right away and shows their current rating.
- New skip button (arrow) on every player: no vote now, rate them later the same day.
- The "never" button is now a small no-entry icon.
- Sharing now always uses the hidden realm-wide channel, so votes reach players outside your guild and group. The separate setting is gone.

## 0.8.5

- Cleaner thumb icons: sharp at small sizes, no more jagged yellow edge.

## 0.8.4

- The minimap button and the addon icon now use the Peer Review logo.

## 0.8.3

- New thumbs up, neutral and thumbs down icons.
- Windows are now solid, so the game world no longer shows through the text.
- Picked reasons are easier to read.

## 0.8.2

- New "Feedback or report a bug" button in the settings, with links you can copy.
- "My ratings" now shows your own rating, as other players have rated you. `/peer me` does the same.
- "Preview popup" replaces the sample popup: see the rating window and drag it where you want it. It opens there from then on.
- Simpler `/peer help`.

## 0.8.1

- New command: `/peer version` shows which version you have installed.

## 0.8.0

First public beta.

- Rating popup when you leave a group: thumbs up, neutral or thumbs down, with up to three optional reasons.
- Only asks about players you were grouped with for 30 minutes (adjustable) or killed a boss with.
- Ratings and reasons in player tooltips, and a private chat warning when a badly rated player joins.
- Votes are shared automatically between Peer Review users in your group and guild.
- A vote is only accepted from the player who cast it. Rage votes are ignored, scores need three voters, votes expire after 180 days, level 20 to vote or be rated.
- "My ratings" window to review, change or remove your votes.
- Friends and guildmates are skipped by default; "never" stops questions about a player for good.
